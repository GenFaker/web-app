TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Know how your guidance is made.

A deterministic foundation with optional AI-assisted explanations.

01

Define the scope first

A full assessment covers five AWS categories. A partial assessment covers the categories you choose. A single-service assessment reviews one named service. Skipped categories are outside scope, never counted as secure or answered.

02

Rules make the decisions

The versioned knowledge base selects applicable covered services, risks, actions, framework mappings, and AWS links. AI may add explanations, but cannot change those structured decisions.

03

Charts show stated plans

Overview charts count service-control entries from a selected saved advisory, marked planned, gap, or unresolved. Detailed reports focus on findings and recommended actions. A planned safeguard is self-reported and unverified. These are not security or compliance scores.

04

Keep the history

Assessment drafts, folders, planning notes, and generated report snapshots are stored on your device. Revising an assessment does not rewrite its previous advisories. API keys stay in session memory.

Research scope and validation

This application gives pre-deployment technical guidance for website hosting on AWS. It does not scan, provision, configure, audit, or certify deployed resources. Organizational policies and compliance judgments remain outside scope.

The checklist, rules, thematic framework mappings, and engineering scenarios are a draft instrument. Qualified AWS expert review, expert benchmarking, usability assessment, and a controlled proof of concept remain capstone research activities.
